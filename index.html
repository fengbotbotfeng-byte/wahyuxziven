Chapter I

The Peaceful Village of Bolaang Satu

Long ago, there was a beautiful village called Bolaang Satu in North Sulawesi. The village was surrounded by green hills, forests, and beautiful rivers. The people lived peacefully and helped one another.

Setting & Scenery Bolaang Satu in North Sulawesi, surrounded by green hills, forests, and beautiful rivers
Community Life The villagers lived peacefully and always helped one another

2

Chapter II

Datu & The Voice in the Forest

One day, a young man named Datu went into the forest to find food. Suddenly, he heard someone calling for help. He followed the voice and found an old man who was lost.

3

Chapter III

The Old Man's Reward

Datu helped the old man return to the village. As a reward, the old man gave him a special message:

“Always respect your land, protect nature, and help other people.” Special Message from the Old Man

Datu shared the message with the villagers. From that day on, the people of Bolaang Satu always worked together, respected their traditions, and protected their village.

4

Closing & Moral Value

Kindness, Tradition & Unity

Finally, Bolaang Satu became a peaceful village known for its kindness and unity.

Moral Value:
We must help others, respect traditions, protect nature, and maintain unity.
